## 🎯 ICP Prioritization (Phase 1)

We will **acquire two segments simultaneously**, both via creator partnerships:

### Segment A: Korean / Indian / SEA professionals writing in English
- **Pain:** Sound fluent, don't want to sound translated
- **Price tolerance:** $15–25/mo
- **Channel:** Korean LinkedIn creators, Indian YouTube English-learning creators, SEA productivity Twitter
- **Acquisition cost ceiling:** $25 (Pro LTV at 12mo = $180)

### Segment B: LinkedIn creators (3k–50k followers)
- **Pain:** Maintain consistent voice across daily posts
- **Price tolerance:** $19–30/mo
- **Channel:** Direct outreach + referral program + Justin Welsh/Lara Acosta style endorsements
- **Acquisition cost ceiling:** $40 (heavier evangelism but better referral multiplier)

**Why these two:** They referral-loop into each other. A Korean LinkedIn creator using Writing Twin teaches their audience to use it, which spreads to Korean professionals. Compound effect.

### Future Segments (Phase 3+)
- Sales reps (Apollo / Outreach / Lemlist communities)
- Customer support managers (Intercom + Zendesk admin communities)
- Job seekers (career subreddits, mid-funnel)
- Enterprise comms (outbound sales motion only)

## 💵 Pricing Mechanics

| Tier | Price | Yearly Discount | Strategic Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | n/a | Acquisition + virality |
| Pro Monthly | $19 | — | Default conversion |
| Pro Yearly | $190 ($15.83/mo) | 17% off | Cash + reduced churn |
| Team (3+ seats) | $39/seat/mo | $390/seat/year (17%) | Expansion + word-of-mouth in orgs |
| Enterprise | $99–200/seat/mo | Custom | High-ACV anchor |

### Pricing Page Psychology
- **Anchor:** Show Team price first (highest visible)
- **Default highlight:** Pro Yearly with "Best value" badge
- **Social proof:** "Used by 2,300+ professionals at [logos]"
- **Risk reversal:** 14-day money-back, no questions
- **Urgency (Phase 2):** "Founding member" pricing locked in for life if signed in first 90 days

### Free Tier Limits (designed to force conversion)
- 30 rewrites/day (enough to feel value, not enough to live on)
- No DNA
- No "Write Like Me"
- 3 basic tones only
- "Powered by Writing Twin AI" appended on output (FREE only, removable on Pro)

## 🧪 Experiments Backlog (Test in this order)

1. **Pricing test:** $19 vs $24 Pro monthly — measure conversion + revenue/visitor
2. **Free tier limit:** 30 vs 20 rewrites/day — measure conv + retention
3. **Onboarding:** DNA-first vs Rewrite-first — measure activation rate
4. **Email subject:** "Your draft is ready" vs "Sounds like you ✨" — open rate
5. **Extension placement:** below subject vs above signature — usage rate
6. **Tone defaults:** Professional vs auto-detect — accept rate
7. **Trial length:** 7-day vs 14-day — conversion rate
8. **Annual discount:** 17% vs 25% — % choosing annual

Pick one per week. Run for 7 days minimum. Ship the winner.

## 🛑 Things That Will Tempt Us and We Should Resist

- ❌ **Going broad too early:** "Add a Slack integration" before Gmail is profitable
- ❌ **Enterprise before $20k MRR:** sales cycle eats 6 months we don't have
- ❌ **Building features for non-paying users:** the free tier exists for acquisition only
- ❌ **Vanity metrics:** signups, downloads, impressions. Only Pro MRR matters.
- ❌ **Generic content marketing:** "10 ways to write a better email" — everyone has this
- ❌ **Paid ads before product-market fit:** until 6% free→Pro conv, ads will burn cash
