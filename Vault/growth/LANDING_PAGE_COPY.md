### Pricing FAQ
**Q: Do I need a credit card to start?**
No. Free tier requires only an email address.

**Q: Can I cancel anytime?**
Yes. Cancel in one click from your account settings. You keep access until the end of the billing period.

**Q: Does Writing Twin read or store my emails?**
No. Writing Twin processes the text you select and send for rewriting. We do not access your inbox, store your emails, or read content you haven't submitted. See our [Privacy Policy](#).

**Q: What languages does it support?**
Writing Twin rewrites in English. Cultural Intelligence adapts the English style for non-native context (Korean hierarchy, Indian idioms, etc.). Multi-language output is on our roadmap.
